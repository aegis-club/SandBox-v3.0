04 Code of conduct

Rules of the playground.

SandBox follows the MLH Code of Conduct. It covers participants, organisers, volunteers, sponsors, judges and mentors.

  • 01

    Respect everyone

    No harassment, in person or online. Treat every person on the floor as a teammate.

  • 02

    Hack responsibly

    Attack only the targets you are given. Anything outside the sandbox stays off limits.

  • 03

    Protect privacy

    Use test data. Never collect, share or publish anyone's personal information.

  • 04

    Speak up

    If something feels wrong, tell an organiser. Reports stay confidential.

To report an incident, write to [incident contact] or visit the help desk at [help desk location].
