# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React + Vite + TypeScript, with Motion (`motion/react`) as the animation library. The user chose React and Motion. Lenis and plain CSS with custom properties are Claude's supporting choices. Tailwind and GSAP are not used.

## Users
College students in India who build software and care about security. They arrive from a poster, a WhatsApp forward, or Instagram, mostly on a phone. They decide in under a minute whether SandBox is worth a team and a night. Sponsors, judges, and mentors are a second audience; they check that the event is serious.

## Product Purpose
The site announces SandBox v3.0, a 24-hour cybersecurity hackathon by Aegis at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE), Bengaluru, in 2027. Success means a visitor understands the event, picks a territory, and intends to register when registration opens.

## Positioning
SandBox is the hackathon where teams build and then attack their own build inside a contained environment. The loop is Build / Break / Experiment / Repeat. It is the third edition.

## Operating Context
The event runs for 24 hours on one campus. It has 8 security "territories" (Z-01 to Z-08) and a 9-phase journey: 3 phases before, 4 inside the sandbox, 2 after. It follows the MLH Code of Conduct.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Registration is not open. The Register action leads to a "Request entry" section that states this.
- Dates for 2027 are not announced.
- Prizes and sponsor names are not known. The site must not invent them.
- Copy comes from the existing static draft (`legacy/index.html`).

## Brand Commitments
- Yellow, or a shade of yellow, is non-negotiable as the brand colour. Claude chooses how yellow, black, and white combine.
- A custom "SANDBOX" wordmark with animation replaces a generic headline. The reference is `inspo/inspo-01.png`.
- The result must not look too playful, and must not look like a corporate SaaS page.
- The user wants to be surprised by something new compared with the v2 site (sandbox.aegisclub.site).

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing copy, territory data, and phase list in the static draft.
- The v2 live site as a reference for expectations.
- No dates, prize amounts, sponsor logos, photos, or testimonials.

## Product Principles
1. State only what is confirmed; mark the rest "to be announced".
2. A phone visitor understands the event in one screen.
3. The site treats security as serious work, not as a costume.
4. Motion carries meaning: containment, breach, release.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Motion respects the reduced-motion setting. Text meets WCAG AA contrast. All controls work with a keyboard.
