# Surface brief: SandBox v3.0 home page

Scope: the single-page event site (`index.html`, `src/`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: student builders on a phone decide in under a minute whether SandBox is worth a team and a night. Sponsors and judges check that the event is serious.
Action: "Request entry". Registration is not open, so the action leads to a section that says so and tells the visitor what to prepare.
Content: copy from the static draft in `legacy/`. Unknown facts read "to be announced". No invented dates, prizes, or sponsors.
Must avoid: a look that is too playful; a corporate SaaS look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SandBox is a row of sealed boxes that break open. The wordmark is seven boxes; carving them open spells SANDBOX. The page refuses the dark-terminal hacker page and the v2 sticker-and-shadow neobrutalist page.

OWN-WORLD: A screen-printed event poster. Signal yellow field (#ffd400) with a faint white grid, warm ink (#14120b), white paper. Three colours only. Full-bleed banner strips that alternate polarity, with rotated end-cap labels and a fringe of fine lines. Archivo variable at width 125 and weight 900 for display; Martian Mono only for Z codes, phase numbers, the version tag, and coordinates. Section headings for Journey, Alliance, and Request entry sit inside strips; a two-strip phrase band replaces the old hazard tape. Square corners, 2px ink rules, no shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor sees the name, learns it is a 24-hour security hackathon at DSCE Bengaluru in 2027, scrolls into the black box ("inside the sandbox"), meets the loop, picks a territory, reads the journey, and reaches "Request entry".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow grid field. Four strips on desktop (five on phones) fill the top 60 percent; each repeats the custom SANDBOX wordmark; row one starts on a full word. Letters start sealed as solid boxes and carve open in row order. Below: headline "24 hours to break things and build them back safer", lead, and the ink "Request entry" button with a line-style "Explore territories".

Signature interaction: on scroll the strips slide apart in opposite directions and an ink box grows from the centre until it fills the screen. Hovering a letter seals it again. Motion grammar: carve, wipe, and stretch; no fade-up on every section.

FORM: Poster strips from the user's reference (inspo/inspo-01.png). seed: none (pinned). Pin: "Keeping yellow or a shade of yellow is non negotiable", "instead of hackathon a custom sanbox with some animation or transition would look really cool". A roll was dealt (seed:2bf31e197a6c8c64) before the pin was recognised; the pin overrode it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: real dates, prizes, sponsors, contacts, and the registration link.
